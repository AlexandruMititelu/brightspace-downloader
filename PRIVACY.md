# Privacy policy

Brightspace Downloader collects no data.

- It talks only to `https://brightspace.tudelft.nl`, using the session you are already
  logged in with, to read the courses you are enrolled in and download their files.
- Downloaded files are written only to the folder you choose on your own computer.
- The only thing it stores is a reference to your chosen default folder, kept locally in
  the browser.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, ads
  or remote code.
